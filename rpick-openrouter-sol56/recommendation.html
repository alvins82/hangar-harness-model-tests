Direct answer on Lo Presti’s

It works as a restaurant for the night: Saturday service runs 12:00pm–11:30pm, 7:00pm sits comfortably inside those hours, it clears the 4.4 Google threshold, the drive is short, and a sensible shared order lands inside $100–$150. It does not yet work as a confirmed plan: its booking route is OpenTable, which blocked the automated table check, so availability remains unconfirmed—not assumed.

The closest thing is Fontana. It is roughly 100 metres away on the same street and matches the important qualities rather than merely the cuisine label: independent ownership, a small upstairs neighbourhood room, regional/produce-conscious Italian cooking and a shareable à-la-carte meal at the same spend. Call Lo Presti’s first; if it cannot seat you, call Fontana.
