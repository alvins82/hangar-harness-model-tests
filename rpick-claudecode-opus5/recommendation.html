I tried the AU restaurant page and the restref availability endpoint from a real browser; both timed out (30.0s and 18.1s) with no response body. There is no second platform to route around to. Close it yourself: the OpenTable listing, or email info@loprestisrestaurant.com as the venue directs for booking problems.

Closest thing to it, if it can't be had: Hamsi Taverna. Lo Presti's is Mediterranean share plates with Greek-leaning hospitality in a ~50 seat neighbourhood room; Hamsi is the same eastern-Mediterranean grammar — mezze, pide, whole fish, plates dressed at the table — and it is bookable at 19:00 right now. It trades the tucked-away Redfern terrace for a much bigger waterfront room, which is the real difference between them.
